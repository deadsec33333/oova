## Concept
A memecoin with a real, free tool attached. Make a pay link, send it to anyone, they pay from any Solana wallet, you get the exact amount in USDC. Wallet to wallet. QOVA never holds money.

Main audience: people in Africa, Latin America and Southeast Asia who want stable digital dollars instead of weak local money. The pitch is for everyone: freelancers, families, small shops, anyone who gets paid from far away.

**Honesty rule for this coin:** $QOVA is a meme. It is not a dollar, not a stablecoin and its price moves. The pay link works without it. This is said near the top of the site and in every pinned post.

## Mascot: Qova
- **What:** a heavy polished chrome coin shaped like the letter Q. The circle is the coin, the tail is the link.
- **Material / medium:** mirror chrome with a slow shine sweep, rendered clean and 3D, never cartoonish.
- **Anatomy twist:** the tail. It stretches across the screen to whoever needs paying, then snaps back.
- **Signature behaviour:** tilts toward your cursor or finger, flips once when a link is made, glows mint for a moment when money lands.
- **Default emotion:** calm, sure of itself. Never shouts.
- **States:** idle (slow shine), made (flip), paid (mint glow), asleep (dim, when the tab is hidden).
- **WOW test:** a circle with a tail. Reads in one second, works at 64 px, a kid draws it in two strokes.

## Logo
The mark is a coin shaped like a chat bubble, cut by the tail of a Q. Coin plus message: a payment request you send like a text. One path, black or white only. Files in `brand/` (SVG marks, app icon, PFP, X banner, wordmark). Wordmark: QOVA in Geist SemiBold, tracking 0.24em.

## World

## Lore (told as short transmissions, one line each)
Every coin in history carried a face. A king, a president, a flag. It told you whose money it was.

Qova was struck with no face. Just a circle, and a tail that points to whoever needs it next.

It does not belong to a country. It does not belong to a bank. It belongs to the link.

When someone far away finishes the work, the tail finds them. When a mother abroad sends home, the tail finds her kids. When a shop in a small town sells to a stranger, the tail finds the shop.

That is all Qova does. It closes the distance between work and pay.
